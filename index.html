Executive Reborn: Built for Real Workplace Fluency Without Textbook Burnout

Executive Reborn replaces dry rote drills with a high-stakes corporate comedy-mystery alongside Marshal, your sharp-witted Shiba companion. Every session connects listening, Cornell-style reading notes, and FSRS flashcard intervals inside an offline app weighing roughly 230 MB.

58 Interactive Episodes

42 main episodes (EP01–EP42) plus 16 side-story visual novel chapters with branching choices and line-by-line native voice playback.

1,397 Curated Phrases

Backed by the New General Service List (NGSL) and TOEIC Service List (TSL), paired with 11,250 AAC word and gloss audio clips.

FSRS Memory Engine

Uses the open-source Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS) to schedule reviews at optimal recall intervals—and never locks words you already learned.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Executive Reborn by Fawnfell?

Executive Reborn is an offline-first business English RPG and FSRS flashcard app built by Fawnfell. It includes 42 main episodes, 16 side-story episodes, 1,397 business phrases, and full audio for working professionals across Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and English.

How much does Executive Reborn cost and is there a lifetime option?

The first 3 main episodes and first 2 side stories are free. You can unlock all 58 episodes via a $4.99/month subscription, a $17.99 Standard Lifetime buyout, or a $24.99 Deluxe Edition with Time Wardrobe unlocks and alternate app icons.

What happens to learned vocabulary if a monthly subscription expires?

Any word you have already learned remains permanently unlocked in your FSRS review queue. Fawnfell guarantees zero lockouts on your personal spaced-repetition study history.

Does Executive Reborn work offline on commutes?

Yes. All 58 story episodes, 1,397 phrases, and 11,250 AAC word audio clips are bundled locally so commuters can study and listen with lock-screen controls without cellular data.
