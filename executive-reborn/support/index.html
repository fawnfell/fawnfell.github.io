Executive Reborn — Support & Help

Need help with Executive Reborn on iOS? Restore purchases in one tap inside the app, review subscription management steps below, or reach Fawnfell directly at fawnfell@gmail.com.

Email Support Privacy Policy
How do I restore purchases after reinstalling or switching iPhones?

Open ME → Subscription & Restore Purchases inside Executive Reborn and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

What happens to my learned words if a monthly subscription ends?

All words you have already studied stay permanently unlocked in your FSRS spaced-repetition review queue, even if your monthly subscription expires.

How do I switch between a monthly subscription and a lifetime buyout?

Purchase the Standard or Deluxe Lifetime plan inside the app, then open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions to cancel the monthly renewal so you are not billed twice.

日本語サポート・購入の復元方法について

機種変更や再インストール時は、アプリ内の「ME」タブから「サブスクリプションと復元」を開き、「購入を復元」をタップしてください。ご不明点は fawnfell@gmail.com までお送りください。
