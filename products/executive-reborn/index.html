How Review Works

Core Learning Model: Multi-Angle Vocabulary Acquisition (Not a Mock-Exam Bank)

Unlike typical English apps that split vocabulary, listening, and reading into three unrelated modules, Executive Reborn was designed from the start around Same-Episode Cross-Modal Recycling (known in second-language acquisition as Narrow Listening & Reading). Every episode binds the exact same 21–29 TSL/NGSL target phrases across three steps: (1) latency-based FSRS-6 warm-up activates meaning, (2) minutes later you hear those same words spoken in the episode's boardroom audio, and (3) immediately afterward you spot them highlighted inside the episode's core memo and linked WORK Desk emails.

Focused TOEIC® Prep Scope: Part 3 Dialogues & Part 7 Business Reading (What Is—and Isn’t—Included)

Instead of acting as an all-parts mock-exam bank, Executive Reborn deliberately concentrates on the two sections professionals struggle with most in real work: extended workplace conversations (40 Part 3 dialogues + 2 Part 4 talks) and business document comprehension (42 core readings + 150 Part 7 workplace documents across 8 genres: email, notice, memo, article, advertisement, report, web page, and letter). It intentionally omits Part 1 photo prompts, Part 2 short Q&A, and Part 5/6 standalone grammar gap-fills, and does not predict or guarantee test scores.

Academic Vocabulary Foundation: What Are TSL 1.2 and NGSL 1.2?

Target vocabulary draws on two open academic wordlists developed by Dr. Charles Browne, Dr. Brent Culligan, and Joseph Phillips under CC BY-SA 4.0: the New General Service List (NGSL 1.2) of 2,809 high-frequency core words (reported on the authors’ official site at ~92% coverage of general English texts) and the TOEIC Service List (TSL 1.2) (reported by its authors at ~98.5%–99% combined coverage of TOEIC® test text when paired with NGSL; restructured into 1,250 core course targets here). Our derived wordlists are openly downloadable at Wordlist Licenses, while all 42 scripts, 150 memos, definitions, and audio clips are original Fawnfell works.

How Does Reaction-Time FSRS-6 Work Without Self-Grading Buttons?

Executive Reborn runs the official FSRS-6 scheduler (90% target retention, 10-minute and 1-day learning steps) without forcing learners to self-rate Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Instead, it grades every card from accuracy plus response latency against your own rolling median (last 200 correct answers under 15 s; 2,000 ms cold-start baseline): answers ≤ 0.6× median schedule as Easy (capped at Good on first encounter), 0.6×–1.5× as Good, > 1.5× as Hard, and misses as Again.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Executive Reborn prevent learner burnout and dropout?

It removes five major drop-off causes: automatic reaction-time FSRS-6 eliminates self-rating fatigue, missed days carry zero streak-reset penalties, a 21.6 min/day workplace story loop pairs with commute audio, every 3 lessons unlock an after-hours visual novel chapter, and learned words stay permanently free to review.

What is Executive Reborn by Fawnfell?

Executive Reborn is an offline-first business English RPG and FSRS flashcard app built by Fawnfell. It includes 42 main episodes, 16 side-story episodes, 1,397 business phrases, and full audio across Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and English.

How much does Executive Reborn cost and is there a lifetime option?

The first 3 main episodes and first 2 side stories are free. You can unlock all 58 episodes via a $4.99/month subscription, a $17.99 Standard Lifetime buyout, or a $24.99 Deluxe Edition with Time Wardrobe unlocks and alternate app icons.

What happens to learned vocabulary if a monthly subscription expires?

Any word you have already learned remains permanently unlocked in your FSRS review queue. Fawnfell guarantees zero lockouts on your personal spaced-repetition study history.

Does Executive Reborn work offline on commutes?

Yes. All 58 story episodes, 1,397 phrases, and 11,250 AAC word audio clips are bundled locally so commuters can study and listen with lock-screen controls without cellular data.

Does Executive Reborn use self-grading flashcard buttons or automatic reaction-time FSRS?

It uses automatic reaction-time grading with FSRS-6 (90% target retention) instead of manual Again/Hard/Good/Easy self-rating buttons. Correctness and response speed relative to your own rolling median latency (last 200 answers) automatically choose Easy, Good, Hard, or Again.

How many times does a learner encounter each word across the 42 episodes? (Simulation)

In a virtual-learner simulation at the recommended 1 episode per 2 days pace (not a human clinical trial), completing 42 episodes takes 84 days (~21.6 min/day for vocabulary and reading), with an average of 11.9 active FSRS flashcard reviews per word plus 4.3 separate contextual pickups inside the episode audio and linked workplace articles (6.6 if articles are read twice)—combining spaced-repetition recall with same-lesson Narrow Listening & Reading.

Which TOEIC® parts does Executive Reborn train—and what does it exclude?

It specifically targets extended workplace listening (40 Part 3 multi-speaker dialogues + 2 Part 4 talks) and Part 7 business reading (42 core passages + 150 workplace memos/reports across 8 genres). It intentionally excludes Part 1 photo descriptions, Part 2 short Q&A, and Part 5/6 isolated grammar drills.

All tiers (including Free Preview, Monthly Pro, and Standard Lifetime) automatically display 12 of the 13 window scenes and 9 matching Marshal outfits whenever your device calendar reaches those dates (4 seasons plus Valentine's Day, Rainy Hydrangea, Tanabata, Mid-Autumn, Halloween, Christmas, New Year's Eve, and New Year).
