Devlog #02 · Akita Marshal × Art Pipeline

The Day Marshal the Akita Lost His Tail—and Why We Removed Chocolates from Valentine's Day

2026-11-25 · Fawnfell Independent Studio · 4 min read

While obsessing over our suit-wearing Akita companion, Marshal, we somehow ended up wrapping a full 42-episode corporate drama, 16-chapter mystery side story, 1,397 workplace phrases, and a latency-based FSRS-6 engine around him in Executive Reborn (by Fawnfell). Here are three real art-pipeline stories from building him: rescuing a clipped tail at 832px, erasing dog-toxic Valentine's chocolates overnight, and packing 13 seasonal window scenes into under 7 MB.

Incident 1: Sliced Right Off at the 832px Canvas Edge

During an asset audit of Marshal's sitting sprite, his curled Akita tail looked suspiciously flat on the right side. The source canvas was only 832px wide, slicing off the fluffy tip right at the border.

We added a --wide right-padding pass across all nine full-pose outfits and locked in an automated test verifying that the rightmost pixel column has zero alpha—no more clipped tails.

Incident 2: Erasing a Toxic Box of Chocolates on Valentine's Day

Our initial Valentine's Day window scene looked gorgeous: pink sunset over Parisian rooftops, a bouquet of roses, and an open box of chocolates right on the rug—with one piece sitting loose beside it.

Then we remembered who sits in the middle of that rug on February 14: Marshal is a dog. Even in illustration form, leaving toxic chocolate right next to our Akita felt wrong, so we ran a targeted Qwen Image-Edit img2img pass to erase the box and keep only dog-safe roses.

Packing 13 Window Scenes & a 10-Year Mid-Autumn Table Under 7 MB

Because Executive Reborn runs completely offline, we kept all 13 window scenes (4 seasons + 8 auto-triggered holidays + 1 Deluxe Typhoon Double Rainbow) plus Marshal's outfits under 7 MB total—complete with a built-in 2026–2035 lunar Mid-Autumn calendar table so the full moon arrives on the exact right date every year.

Bonus Plot Twist: Catching a 'Shiba' Typo Across Our Own Site

And just today (November 25, 2026), we caught an embarrassing handoff slip: despite our story bible explicitly specifying an Akita, earlier website metadata and alt tags labeled Marshal a 'Shiba Inu'. We scrubbed every language page, Open Graph tag, and llms-full.txt back to Akita—and locked it into our handoff docs.
