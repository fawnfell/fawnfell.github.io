Why Flashcard Apps Fail in 3 Weeks: Replacing 4 Manual Rating Buttons with Reaction-Latency FSRS-6

2026-11-25 · Fawnfell Independent Studio · 4 min read

It is 10 p.m. after a long commute, and tapping Again / Hard / Good / Easy a hundred times in a row burns out your willpower faster than the vocabulary itself. In Executive Reborn—Fawnfell's offline business English RPG (58 episodes, 1,397 workplace phrases, 11,250 AAC voice clips)—we deleted all four manual grading buttons and drive FSRS-6 automatically from accuracy and each learner's rolling 200-item median response latency.

Why Manual 4-Button Self-Grading Causes Burnout

Traditional spaced-repetition apps ask a meta-cognitive question on every single card: 'Was that Hard or Good?' Repeating that judgment 100 times at night turns review into tedious clerical work.

Executive Reborn removes self-rating entirely. You simply pick the meaning; the engine compares your response speed against your own rolling 200-answer median and accuracy to assign the FSRS-6 grade automatically (targeting 90% retention).

Closing the Loop with Same-Episode Narrow Input

Instead of isolating flashcards from audio and reading, each episode locks a single batch of 21–29 target phrases: FSRS-6 warm-up first, then corporate meeting audio minutes later, then workplace documents with soft-glow term highlights.

In our simulated learner model (one episode every two days, ~21.6 minutes/day), each phrase gets an average of 11.9 active FSRS reviews plus 4.3 contextual encounters (6.6 on a second read-through)—a dual-track design grounded in Narrow Listening & Reading (simulation metric, not a score guarantee).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does removing manual rating buttons hurt FSRS-6 accuracy?

No. Comparing response time against your personal 200-item median separates instant recall from hesitant guesses without decision fatigue.

What happens if I skip a week during a busy sprint?

There is no streak reset, and the queue is simulation-tested to converge smoothly even after 14-day pauses.

Can I still review learned words if a subscription ends?

Yes. Your FSRS review queue for learned vocabulary remains unlocked forever on every tier, alongside a $17.99 Standard Lifetime purchase option.
